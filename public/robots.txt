# AI crawlers are allowed to fetch this site. Search, user-fetch, and training
# tokens are named so the policy is visible. Every group below is Allow: /.
# None of these agents are disallowed.
#
# What differs is the token, not the directive:
# - OAI-SearchBot, Claude-SearchBot, and PerplexityBot are the search indexes
#   behind ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity.
# - ChatGPT-User, Claude-User, and Perplexity-User fetch a page when a person
#   asks a question. Their vendors say those user agents may not honor
#   robots.txt. They are still allowed here.
# - GPTBot, ClaudeBot, and CCBot collect pages that may be used for training.
# - Google-Extended and Applebot-Extended are training opt-out tokens. They do
#   not fetch pages. Allowing them lets Gemini and Apple Intelligence use the
#   content for training. Google Search and AI Overviews use Googlebot.
#   Apple search uses Applebot. Both stay allowed under User-agent: *.
#   Disallowing Google-Extended would not remove AI Overviews.
# - Any agent not named here, including other training crawlers, is allowed
#   by User-agent: *. Markdown alternates (/*.md) are allowed too. They are
#   kept out of the index with X-Robots-Tag: noindex, not with Disallow.
